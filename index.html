Aims & Scope

IJMAHS exists because allied health training rarely gets the depth of scholarly attention given to medicine and nursing alone. The journal covers the full breadth of allied health programs and courses — their science, their teaching, and their clinical practice.

DIAGNOSTIC & THERAPEUTIC

Operation Theatre Technology, Anaesthesia Technology, Dialysis Technology, Radiography & Imaging, Medical Laboratory Technology.

REHABILITATION & CARE

Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Nursing, Optometry, Audiology & Speech-Language Pathology.

EDUCATION & ASSESSMENT

Competency-based curricula, clinical internship logbooks, OSCE and skills assessment, simulation-based training.

CLINICAL PRACTICE

Infection control & aseptic technique, patient safety, procedural protocols, allied health scope-of-practice studies.

HEALTH SYSTEMS

Workforce planning, allied health policy, quality improvement in diagnostic and therapy services.

EDUCATIONAL INNOVATION

Teaching methods, MCQ item design, digital learning tools, textbook and courseware development.

Peer Review Process

Every submission that passes editorial screening goes through double-blind peer review before a decision is issued.

01

Editorial screening

The editorial office checks scope, format, and completeness — typically within 5–7 days of submission.

02

Reviewer assignment

Two or more independent reviewers are assigned; author identity is withheld from reviewers and vice versa.

03

Review & report

Reviewers evaluate methodology, originality, and clinical or educational relevance, typically within 3–4 weeks.

04

Editorial decision

Accept, minor revision, major revision, or reject — communicated with reviewer comments to the corresponding author.

05

Revision & re-review

Revised manuscripts are returned to original reviewers where feasible before a final decision.

06

Acceptance & APC

On acceptance, the corresponding author is invoiced the Article Processing Charge prior to publication.

Indexing & Discoverability

IJMAHS is a new title, so this page states its indexing status exactly as it stands rather than claim coverage before it exists. A journal advertising immediate Scopus or PubMed listing on day one is one of the clearest predatory-journal warning signs — the databases below only evaluate a title after it has a real publication record.

DOAJApplying at launch

Directory of Open Access Journals — application submitted once Volume 1, Issue 1 is published.

CrossrefDOIs from Issue 1

Every accepted article is registered for a Crossref DOI starting with the inaugural issue.

Google ScholarSite metadata live

The site carries standard scholarly page metadata; per-article citation tags ship with each published issue so Scholar can crawl and index them.

ROAD / ISSN PortalPending ISSN

Listed in the ISSN Portal's open-access directory as soon as the journal's ISSN is issued.

Scopus & Web of ScienceLong-term target

Both require 2–3 years of regular, quality-reviewed publishing before an application is even accepted.

PubMed / MEDLINELong-term target

NLM selection requires an established peer-reviewed publication history — pursued once IJMAHS has one.

Authors: verify any journal's indexing claims directly on the indexer's own site before submitting or paying — never take a journal's word alone, including ours.
